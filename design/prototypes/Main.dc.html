Two engines, one app

ENGINE 1 · YOUR STREAMS

Grow your own channel

After every stream, the app finds your best moments, cuts them to 9:16 with your facecam on top, adds captions, and suggests a title. You review a short queue and publish to Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

The goal: every stream turns into 3 to 8 posts without you scrubbing the VOD.

ENGINE 2 · OTHER CREATORS

Clip for others, behind the scenes

Creators post clipping campaigns: "pay $[RATE] per 1,000 views on clips of my streams." The app watches their new VODs in the background, drafts clips, and you post the good ones on your clip pages and get paid per view.

The goal: income and audience-building while your own channel is still small.

Finding moments without a big chat

Most clippers lean on chat spikes, which barely exist for a small streamer. ClipForge scores every second of a VOD from signals that come from the streamer, not the audience, then fuses them.

Voice energyLoudness spikes, laughter, screams and sudden silence in your mic track.
Transcript readingWhisper transcribes, then an LLM flags quotable lines, stories, rage and punchlines.
Game eventsKills, clutches, wins and deaths from game APIs or by reading the HUD.
Your markersA hotkey or Stream Deck button you press live. The strongest signal there is.
Facecam reactionBig expression changes and leaning into the camera.
Chat, when there is oneMessage bursts and emotes add weight, but are never required.

Clipping others, the way that lasts

Reposting someone's stream without permission breaks copyright and platform rules, and gets clip accounts banned. So Engine 2 only works from sources a creator has opted into.

  • Campaigns: creators list their channel, a pay rate per 1,000 views, and rules (no spoilers, must tag them, banned topics).
  • Credit built in: every campaign clip carries the creator's name and link, and the payout tracking that comes with it.
  • Takedown in one click: a creator can pull any clip or end a campaign, and the app removes the posts.
  • View checks: payouts only count verified views, so botted views don't pay.
